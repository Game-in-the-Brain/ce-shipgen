# Factions & Legal Framework — Mneme Variant

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-05-01  
**Scope:** Major political entities, their attitudes toward armed vessels, concealed weapons, and merchant protection.

---

## Faction Overview

The Mneme Variant replaces traditional Traveller factions with original political entities. Each has distinct attitudes toward armed merchant vessels, concealed weapons, and naval architecture.

---

## 1. The Houses

*Replaces: Imperial Space*

### Identity
Corporate-bloodline elites who hold power across multiple star systems. The Houses are not a unified empire — they are a loose aristocracy of merchant-princes, industrial dynasties, and banking families who trace their legitimacy to ancient charters and monopolies.

### Government
Plutocratic oligarchy. Each House rules its systems as private property. The High Council mediates disputes between Houses but has no standing military of its own.

### Naval Doctrine
The Houses maintain private fleets masquerading as "merchant protection services." Q-ships are common — a House trader is expected to defend itself. Letters of Marque are issued freely to House vessels operating in contested systems.

### Concealed Weapons Law

| Status | Requirement | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **House Vessel** | Automatic permit | All House ships may conceal weapons |
| **Licensed Contractor** | Letter of Marque | Annual renewal. 1 concealed turret per 200 DT |
| **Independent Merchant** | House Charter | Expensive. Requires House sponsor |
| **Foreign Vessel** | Temporary Permit | 30-day limit. Random inspection |

### Q-Ship Status
- **With Letter of Marque:** Lawful combatant
- **Without papers:** Piracy (penalty: seizure + indentured servitude)

### Port Protocol
House ports scan all vessels on approach. Concealed weapons detected without permit = immediate impound + heavy fine.

## 2. The Hegemony

*Replaces: Aslan Hierate*

### Identity
Warring States composed of martial clans. The Hegemony has no central government — it is a perpetual battlefield of competing warlords, each commanding a fleet of clan ships. Honor and strength are the only currencies.

### Government
Clan-based military hierarchy. The High Warlord is elected by victory in combat, not bloodline. Clans rise and fall with their fleets.

### Naval Doctrine
Every Hegemony vessel is a warship first. Even cargo ships carry weapons — a clan that cannot defend its merchantmen is a clan that starves. Hegemony designs favor **Brawlers** — armor and firepower over speed.

### Concealed Weapons Law

| Status | Requirement | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Clan Vessel** | No permit required | All ships expected to be armed |
| **Bonded Mercenary** | Clan Honor Mark | Serves one clan for contract term |
| **Independent** | Challenge Right | Must defeat a clan champion in single ship combat to earn passage |
| **Foreign Vessel** | Tribute or Combat | Pay cargo tribute or fight your way through |

### Q-Ship Status
- **With Honor Mark:** Respected tactic. Deception is part of war.
- **Without clan affiliation:** Target of opportunity. No legal protection.

### Port Protocol
Hegemony ports do not scan — they **challenge**. A ship entering clan space must identify its clan or declare itself independent (which is an invitation to attack). Concealed weapons are assumed. The only question is whether you have the strength to use them.

### Special Rule: Surprise and Honor
Hegemony clans view **surprise attacks on merchants** as dishonorable. However, **surprise attacks on warships** are considered skillful. A Q-ship that reveals itself to attack a Hegemony warcruiser gains respect. A Q-ship that ambushes a Hegemony grain hauler gains a blood feud.

## 3. The Guilds

*Replaces: Zhodani Consulate*

### Identity
Techno-administrative elites organized into gene-castes. The Guilds believe social order is maintained through genetic specialization — bred administrators, bred engineers, bred soldiers. They view unregulated armed vessels as threats to social stability.

### Government
Technocracy administered by the Administrator Caste. The Genetic Council determines breeding rights and caste assignments. Dissent is treated as a genetic defect.

### Naval Doctrine
The Guilds maintain a professional navy of standardized designs. Guild vessels are **Vanguards** — balanced, reliable, interchangeable. Individual initiative is discouraged. All weapons must be registered, calibrated, and visibly marked.

### Concealed Weapons Law

| Status | Requirement | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Guild Vessel** | Weapons Manifest | All weapons logged in central registry |
| **Licensed Merchant** | Disarmament Bond | Must surrender weapons at Guild port. Returned on departure |
| **Independent** | **Prohibited** | No independent may carry weapons in Guild space |
| **Foreign Military** | Diplomatic Clearance | Weapon systems sealed. Break seal = act of war |

### Q-Ship Status
- **In Guild Space:** **Illegal.** Treated as terrorism. Crew subject to re-education or liquidation.
- **Outside Guild Space:** Guild navy will hunt and destroy known Q-ships as threats to order.

### Port Protocol
Guild ports use **deep scan technology**. Concealed weapons are detected automatically. Vessels with unregistered weapons are boarded, crews processed for caste re-assignment or termination.

### Special Rule: Psionic Scanning
Guild ports employ psionic screening (where setting allows). A concealed weapon detected by a psionic scan carries **double penalties** — not only illegal armament, but also "attempted deception of the state."

## 4. The Federation / Alliance

*Replaces: Solomani Confederation / Hiver Federation*

### Identity
A loose coalition of democratic states with diverse governance models — direct democracies, republics, corporate charters, even some monarchies. The Federation is held together by trade agreements and mutual defense pacts rather than centralized authority.

### Government
Federal council with rotating presidency. Member states retain sovereignty over internal affairs. The Federation Navy is a combined force contributed by member states.

### Naval Doctrine
The Federation believes in **free navigation** and **merchant self-defense**. Armed merchants are common. Federation designs tend toward **Vanguards** and **Haulers** — multi-role vessels that can trade in peace and defend in war.

### Concealed Weapons Law

| Status | Requirement | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Federation Vessel** | Merchant Defense License | Annual permit. Random inspection |
| **Member State Vessel** | State Registration | Varies by member state |
| **Independent Merchant** | Port-of-Entry Permit | Issued per system. Inexpensive |
| **Foreign Vessel** | Transit Visa | 90-day limit. No concealed weapons without license |

### Q-Ship Status
- **With Merchant Defense License:** Lawful. Must identify self if challenged by Federation Navy.
- **Without license:** Civilian piracy. Fine + confiscation.
- **Posing as Federation vessel:** Espionage. Military tribunal.

### Port Protocol
Federation ports use **standard commercial scan**. Concealed weapons detected = impound until license verified. No license = fine + weapon seizure + possible ban from Federation space.

### Special Rule: Member State Variation
Federation law sets a **minimum standard** — member states may be more permissive but not more restrictive. Some member states allow any merchant to carry any weapon. Others require all weapons to be visible. The Merchant Defense License is valid across all Federation space regardless of local variation.

## 5. The Mneme Frontier

*Setting-specific region*

### Identity
The frontier region where the Mneme Variant takes place. No single faction dominates. It is a patchwork of House enclaves, Hegemony clan territories, Guild outposts, Federation colonies, and independent worlds.

### Government
None at regional level. Individual systems govern themselves — some are democracies, some are corporate fiefs, some are military dictatorships, some are anarchic freeports.

### Naval Doctrine
Everything is armed. A merchant without weapons is a victim waiting to happen. The frontier produces the widest variety of ship designs — from racing couriers to asteroid fortresses.

### Concealed Weapons Law

**System-to-system.** The Frontier has no unified law. Each system sets its own rules:

| System Type | Concealed Weapons | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| **House Enclave** | Permit required (Letter of Marque) | House law applies |
| **Freeport** | **No restrictions** | Anarchy. Every ship for itself |
| **Independent Dictatorship** | Varies by warlord | Check before landing |
| **Former Guild World** | Often illegal | Old Guild scanners still active |
| **Unclaimed** | **No law** | Complete anarchy |

> **Mneme Rule:** There is no "Frontier Law." There is only the law of the system you are currently in, and the space between systems has no law at all.

### Q-Ship Status
- **Everywhere:** Legal. Expected. The question is not "are you armed?" but "how armed?"

### Port Protocol
Varies wildly by system:
- **House enclaves:** Scan + permit check
- **Freeports:** No scan. Pay docking fee. Mind your own business.
- **Independent dictatorships:** Whatever the local warlord says
- **Former Guild worlds:** May still use Guild scanners. Old habits die hard.
