# Mneme Space Combat — Range, Sensors, and Encounter Setup

> **Source:** Mneme Variant Space Combat Rules v2.45

---

## 1. Space Combat Range Bands

Ranges are relative to the PC's ship.

| Range | Distance | Sensor Details | Weapon Notes |
|-------|----------|----------------|--------------|
| **Adjacent (AD)** | <1 km | Individual sources of neural activity (Very Advanced sensors only) | DM+6 for Sensor/Comm Actions or Automatic detection |
| **Close (CL)** | 1 to 9.9 km | Individual ship systems, level of neural activity (Very Advanced sensors only) | DM+4 for Sensor/Comm Actions or Automatic Detection |
| **Short (SH)** | 10 km to 999 km (1 Space) | Fine visual details, individual heat sources, internal structure (Advanced and Very Advanced sensors only) | **Sand Casters Max Range (DM-2)**<br>**Pulse Lasers Max Range (DM-2)**<br>DM+2 for Sensor/Comm Actions |
| **Medium** | 1,000 km to 9,999 km (2-9 Space) | Source of EM emissions, external structure (Advanced and Very Advanced sensors only) | **Beam Lasers Max Range (DM-2)**<br>**Fusion Guns Max Range (DM-2)**<br>DM+0 for Sensor/Comm Actions |
| **Long** | 10K to 24.9K km (10-24 Space) | Ship configuration and shape, thermal activity, external structure (Advanced and Very Advanced sensors only) | DM-2 for Sensor Actions |
| **Very Long** | 25K to 49.9K km (25-49 Space) | Ship's presence and level of activity, basic object silhouette | **Particle Beam Max Range (DM-2)**<br>DM-4 for Sensor/Comm Actions |
| **Distant** | 50+ Space | Only Ships and Ports with Transponder/IFF On can be detected (automatic, limited to speed of light) | **Missiles Max Range**<br>DM-6 for Sensor/Comm Actions |

### Weapon Range Modifiers

- Weapons have **DM+2** when used **closer** than their noted range band
- Weapons have **DM-2** when used **1 band further** than their noted range
- Weapons **cannot be used beyond 1 band more** than their noted range

### Greater than Distant Range

The scale shifts to:
- **Light Seconds:** 300,000 km
- **Light Minutes:** 18M km increments
- **Astronomical Units:** 8 light minutes or 150M km
- **Light Hours:** 1B km

What can be done at these ranges is usually at the Referee's discretion.

## 2. Sensors

### Sensors DM at Ranges

Sensors are less effective at greater ranges:

| Range | Sensor DM |
|-------|-----------|
| Adjacent | +6 |
| Close | +4 |
| Short | +2 |
| Medium | +0 |
| Long | -2 |
| Very Long | -4 |
| Distant | -6 |
| Beyond Distant | -8 and greater (exponential penalties) |

### Object Size Sensor Modifiers

| Size | DM | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Man-Sized | -4 | A Missile/Drone, or Probe |
| 1 DT | -2 | A small vehicle or craft |
| 100 DT | 0 | A Ship |
| 100K DT | +2 | A Capital Ship, Space Station, or Asteroid 1km in diameter |
| 100M DT | +4 | An asteroid around 10km in diameter (e.g., Phobos and Deimos) |
| 100B DT | +6 | An asteroid around 100km in diameter (e.g., Pallas, Vesta, Hygiea) |
| 100T DT | +8 | A planetoid around 1K km in diameter (e.g., the Moon, Pluto, Ganymede) |

### Active and Passive Sensors

- **Active Sensors:** Emit signals to detect objects. Easier to detect but more effective.
- **Passive Sensors:** Only receive signals. Harder to detect but less effective.

### Hostile Intent

Ships that detect other ships must determine if they are hostile, neutral, or friendly. This affects encounter setup and available actions.

## 3. Transponders

- Ships use **transponders** to broadcast their location and announce friendly or neutral intent
- Detection of ships with **active transponders** is automatic at well beyond normal sensor range (limited to speed of light)
- Ships without transponders or with transponders off are harder to detect

---

## 4. Going Dark

A ship can choose to **go dark** — reducing its EM signature to avoid detection:
- Shut down non-essential systems
- Reduce thrust
- Disable transponder
- Going dark makes the ship much harder to detect but limits its own sensor effectiveness and maneuverability

---

## 5. Tight Beam Communication

Directed communication beams that are harder to intercept than broadcast communications. Used for secure ship-to-ship or ship-to-base communication.

---

## 6. Broadcast (Communications) Stance

Ships can choose their communication stance:
- **Open Broadcast:** Anyone can hear
- **Tight Beam:** Directed at specific target
- **Silent:** No transmissions

---

## 7. Encounter Types

### Near Planet Space

Encounters near planets have:
- Shorter starting ranges (typically Short or Medium)
- More traffic and potential contacts
- Planetary defenses may be present

### Interplanetary Encounter

Encounters in deep space:
- Longer starting ranges (typically Long or Very Long)
- Less traffic
- No planetary assistance

---

## 8. Encounter Setup

### 1. Ambush-Quarry Encounters

One side is the **Ambusher**, the other is the **Quarry**.

#### Ambush-Quarry Mechanics

- The ambusher attempts to surprise the quarry
- Roll opposed checks (Stealth vs Sensors, or Tactics vs Tactics)
- Success means the ambusher gets the drop on the quarry

#### Ambush-Quarry Difficulty

| Circumstance | Modifier |
|--------------|----------|
| Quarry is going dark | +2 to ambush |
| Quarry has active sensors | -2 to ambush |
| Ambusher has superior sensors | +1 to ambush |
| Quarry is in asteroid field | +1 to ambush (for ambusher) or -1 (for quarry) |

### 2. Chance Encounters

Random encounters in space.

#### Chance Encounter Mechanics

- Determine starting range based on location (Near Planet = Short/Medium, Interplanetary = Long/Very Long)
- Both sides roll sensors to detect each other
- If one side detects first, they may choose to engage, evade, or observe

---

## 9. Referee Guidance

### Referee Qualifies and Lies, or the Players Figure It Out

The Referee should:
- Describe what sensors show without necessarily revealing full truth
- Allow players to deduce enemy capabilities from sensor data
- Use sensor uncertainty to create tension

## 10. Thrust Points

Thrust rating determines:
- Initiative bonus (side with higher lowest thrust gets +1)
- Maneuverability
- Ability to close or open range

See CE SRD for thrust calculation.
